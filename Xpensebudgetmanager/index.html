01 — English

Xpense Budget Manager ("the App") is a personal expense and income tracker developed by an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles, where it goes, and how you can remove it. It describes version 1.0.4 and later; how earlier versions differ is explained under Earlier versions (1.0.3 and below).

No account, no sign-in

The App does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email, or any identifying information to use it. There is no login screen and no user profile on any server we operate.

Your financial records: on your device, synced only to your own iCloud

Your income and expense entries, budgets, categories, notes, recurring plans, subscription schedules, and payment confirmations are stored on your device. When your iCloud account is available and the device has an internet connection, Apple's CloudKit framework can also sync these records to the private iCloud database tied to your Apple Account.

This sync is automatic, opportunistic, and private: records that finish syncing may be restored after reinstalling or on another device signed in with the same Apple Account. We cannot see, access, or receive a copy of this financial data through any server we operate or our developer portal. The App can continue storing entries locally while offline; changes that have not yet synced are not backed up by this sync.

Signing out of iCloud, disabling iCloud for the App, or switching Apple Accounts can change which records are available locally while Apple updates the sync state. Do not rely on a local copy surviving an account change: export important records before changing iCloud settings, allow time for changes to sync, and check the App again afterward. The iCloud indicator in Settings shows account availability; it does not confirm internet connectivity or that every pending record has uploaded.

The App requests complete file protection (NSFileProtectionComplete) for its local store, which helps protect the stored file while the device is locked.

Receipt scanning

If you use receipt scanning, the image you take or select is processed on your device using Apple's Vision framework to suggest an amount, date, and merchant name. The App does not upload or permanently store the receipt image. It uses the image in memory for scan review and does not retain it as part of the saved financial record. Recognition can be wrong; review the suggestions before saving.

Advertising

Ads keep Xpense free. The App shows banner ads (including inline 300×250 MREC banners) on some screens, such as Home, Add, Analytics and Subscriptions; a full-screen (interstitial) ad every now and then after you save entries; and a rewarded video that you can choose to watch to export a CSV report (see the Terms of Service). There are no in-app purchases, and no setting turns ads off.

Ads come from ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize, through Unity LevelPlay. Each company handles the data it receives under its own privacy notice:

  • ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay: Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (Unity)
  • Liftoff Monetize (formerly Vungle): Privacy Policy (Liftoff)

With either advertising choice, these companies receive technical data such as your IP address, device type and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Your records, receipts, notes and iCloud data are not shared with them, and we do not intentionally send them to advertising partners. If you include any of these in a support email, we receive what you choose to send.

The ad software starts only after you have made your advertising choice. From then on, it prepares ads in advance each time the App is open, even when none is shown.

Your advertising choice

After you accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy on the first screen, and finish the short setup, the App shows a screen called Ads in Xpense. It cannot be closed without a choice, and the ad software does not start until you make one. It has two buttons of the same size, Allow personalized ads and Continue with limited ads:

  • Personalized: ads can be based on your activity in other apps and websites, using your device's advertising ID. iOS may ask whether Xpense may track you.
  • Limited: Xpense tells the ad companies not to personalize your ads and does not ask to track you. You still see ads, but they may be less relevant.

Limited means the App asks for ads that are not personalized and never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. It does not stop data being sent: with either choice, the ad companies still receive the technical data described under Advertising, such as your IP address, device type and how you interact with ads.

With Personalized, the App tells the ad companies you agree to personalized ads and asks iOS for tracking permission, only if iOS has never asked about Xpense before. If you allow it, they can use your advertising ID. If you decline, or iOS does not allow tracking, your choice is saved as Limited and the App continues with limited ads.

ATT is a separate iOS setting. Choosing Limited does not take back a tracking permission you already gave in iOS; to take it back, turn Xpense off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you chose Personalized and later turn tracking off in iOS, your choice in the App stays Personalized, but iOS no longer gives the ad companies your advertising ID, so ads stay limited. The advertising privacy screen then shows a note with an Open Settings button; choose Limited there if you want the ad companies told that you do not agree.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy and iCloud → Advertising privacy. Your new choice applies to ads at the latest the next time you open Xpense. The App keeps your choice only on this device, with the date you made it and the version of the text you saw; this record is not synced to iCloud and is not sent to us. If you delete and reinstall the App, you will normally be asked again.

In version 1.0.4, everyone is asked to choose once more, including people who chose in an earlier version.

If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before you choose.

Before the ad software starts, the App sends it two signals. The first, sent for everyone whichever choice you make, asks the ad companies not to sell or share your personal information (the opt-out signal used under California privacy law); they handle it under their own policies. The second is your choice: with Personalized, that you agree to personalized ads; with Limited, that you do not. If you change your choice later, the new value is sent as soon as you change it.

What the App Store privacy label lists

Our App Store privacy label lists nine data types for the App. Apart from Customer Support, they are collected by the ad networks' software, not by the App's own code. They are based on our own review of the App, on the privacy manifests that come with the ad software, and on the ad networks' published summaries, so they describe what may be collected, not that every type is collected in every session:

  • Device ID — such as your device's advertising ID (only if you allow tracking in iOS) and identifiers the ad software uses. Linked to you; used to track you.
  • Advertising Data — such as the ads you have seen. Linked to you; used to track you.
  • Coarse Location — an approximate location the ad networks may work out, for example from your IP address. The App never asks iOS for your location. Linked to you; used to track you.
  • Product Interaction — how you interact with ads, for example whether an ad was shown or tapped. Not linked to you; not used to track you.
  • Performance Data — how the ad software runs. Linked to you; not used to track you.
  • Other Diagnostic Data — other technical data about the ad software. Not linked to you; not used to track you.
  • Other Data Types — other technical data the ad software collects, such as language or device model. Linked to you; not used to track you.
  • User ID — an ID the ad software assigns (the App has no accounts). Linked to you; not used to track you.
  • Customer Support — emails you choose to send us, including a "Report an ad" email. Linked to you; not used to track you.

Depending on the type, the label gives the purposes as third-party advertising, analytics and/or app functionality; User ID and Customer Support are used only for app functionality. If you choose Personalized and allow tracking in the ATT prompt, the ad networks may link Device ID, Advertising Data and Coarse Location with data from other companies' apps and websites for targeted advertising and ad measurement. The App has no in-app purchases, so the label does not list purchase history.

Reporting an ad

If you see an ad that is not appropriate, use Settings → Privacy and iCloud → Advertising privacy → Report an ad. It opens an email to us that you can edit before you send it. It contains a question about which ad you saw and where, followed by the App version, build number and iOS version, and nothing else about you or your phone.

Notifications

If you allow it, the App schedules enabled subscription, recurring-entry, budget, and periodic reminders locally. A recurring income or expense reminder does not itself create a financial record: a due entry is added only after you confirm it in the App. Daily entry reminders are off by default; when enabled, the App skips today's reminder if an entry has already been recorded when it schedules it. User-visible reminders use the App's selected language and are generated and delivered on your device; no server sends reminder content. CloudKit may use an invisible background system signal to refresh your private iCloud sync; it is not a user notification and carries no reminder content.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete All Data removes your financial records from this device after confirmation. When iCloud sync is available, the App sends those deletions to your private iCloud database through Apple's automatic synchronization; this may take time and cannot be confirmed instantly while offline. The action cannot be undone locally. To remove an iCloud copy directly, use iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Xpense Budget Manager → Delete Data.

Simply deleting the App from your device removes its local copy but does not itself delete records already synced to iCloud.

Your rights and choices

You can view and correct your financial entries in the App and export supported records as CSV through the report feature. For deletion, use Settings → Delete All Data and allow iCloud to sync, or remove the iCloud copy directly as described above. Advertising partners process their own technical data under their own privacy notices; you can change the App's advertising choice at any time from Settings → Privacy and iCloud → Advertising privacy.

If you email us for support, or send a "Report an ad" email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in the message or attachments. This correspondence is handled through our email provider, separately from your in-app financial records, and we do not pass it to advertising partners. Please avoid sending sensitive financial details or receipt images unless needed to explain the issue. You can ask us about deleting a support conversation at the contact address below.

Not financial advice

The App is a personal bookkeeping tool. It does not provide financial, tax or accounting advice and is not an official accounting or banking service.

Data retention

We do not operate a backend database for the financial records you enter in the App. Those records are stored on your device and, when syncing is available, in your private iCloud database. Advertising partners may separately retain the technical advertising data described above under their own privacy policies; the App's Delete All Data action does not directly erase data held by those partners.

Earlier versions (1.0.3 and below)

Versions 1.0.3 and below work as described in this policy, with these differences. They also used Yandex Ads, through Unity LevelPlay, as a fourth ad network, and they contained AppMetrica, Yandex's analytics and crash-reporting component, which Yandex Mobile Ads brought in and started automatically for its ad service. AppMetrica also has a revenue-tracking feature; the App itself reported no purchase or revenue events and has no purchases. Their App Store privacy label therefore also listed crash data, and AppMetrica's own declarations included purchase history as a possible category. In those versions, a changed advertising choice applied immediately to new ad requests. Yandex handles data under its own notice: Yandex (including AppMetrica). Version 1.0.4 no longer contains Yandex Ads or AppMetrica; once you update, this section no longer applies to you.

Kişiselleştirilmemiş seçenek, Uygulamanın kişiselleştirilmemiş reklam istediği ve Apple'ın Uygulama İzleme Şeffaflığı (ATT) isteminin hiç gösterilmediği anlamına gelir. Kişiselleştirilmiş reklamları seçip ATT isteminde izlemeye izin verirsen reklam ağları, Cihaz Kimliği, Reklam Verileri ve Yaklaşık Konum'u hedefli reklamlar ve reklam ölçümü için başka şirketlerin uygulama ve web sitelerindeki verilerle ilişkilendirebilir. Uygulamada uygulama içi satın alma bulunmadığı için etikette satın alma geçmişi yer almaz.

Reklam bildirme

Uygunsuz bir reklam görürsen Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve iCloud → Reklam gizliliği → Reklam bildir yolunu kullan. Göndermeden önce düzenleyebileceğin, bize yazılmış bir e-posta açılır. E-postada hangi reklamı ve nerede gördüğünü soran bir soru ile onun ardından Uygulama sürümü, build numarası ve iOS sürümü bulunur; seninle veya telefonunla ilgili başka bir şey yoktur.

Bildirimler

İzin verirsen, Uygulama etkinleştirdiğin abonelik, tekrarlayan işlem, bütçe ve dönemsel hatırlatmaları yerel olarak planlar. Tekrarlayan gelir/gider hatırlatması kendi başına finansal kayıt oluşturmaz; vadesi gelen işlem yalnızca Uygulamada onayladığında kaydedilir. Günlük kayıt hatırlatması varsayılan olarak kapalıdır; açarsan uygulama planlama sırasında bugün kayıt olduğunu görürse bugünkü hatırlatmayı atlar. Kullanıcıya görünen hatırlatmalar uygulamada seçtiğin dilde ve cihazında oluşturulup iletilir; hiçbir sunucu hatırlatma içeriği göndermez. CloudKit, özel iCloud eşzamanlamasını yenilemek için görünmeyen bir arka plan sistem sinyali kullanabilir; bu kullanıcı bildirimi değildir ve hatırlatma içeriği taşımaz.

Verini silmek

Ayarlar → Tüm Verileri Sil, onaydan sonra finansal kayıtlarını bu cihazdan siler. iCloud eşzamanlaması kullanılabiliyorsa uygulama bu silmeleri Apple'ın otomatik eşzamanlamasıyla özel iCloud veritabanına iletir; işlem zaman alabilir ve çevrimdışıyken anında tamamlandığı doğrulanamaz. Yerel silme geri alınamaz. iCloud kopyasını doğrudan silmek için iOS Ayarlar → [Adın] → iCloud → Hesap Depolamasını Yönet → Xpense Budget Manager → Veriyi Sil yolunu kullanabilirsin.

Uygulamayı cihazından kaldırmak yerel kopyayı siler, fakat iCloud'a daha önce eşzamanlanmış kayıtları tek başına silmez.

Haklarının ve tercihlerin

Finansal kayıtlarını uygulamada görebilir ve düzeltebilir; desteklenen kayıtları rapor özelliğiyle CSV olarak dışa aktarabilirsin. Silmek için Ayarlar → Tüm Verileri Sil'i kullanıp iCloud eşzamanlamasına zaman tanı veya iCloud kopyasını yukarıdaki yoldan doğrudan sil. Reklam ortakları kendi teknik verilerini kendi gizlilik bildirimlerine göre işler; uygulamanın reklam tercihini istediğin zaman Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve iCloud → Reklam gizliliği bölümünden değiştirebilirsin.

Destek için bize e-posta gönderirsen ya da "Reklam bildir" e-postası yollarsan e-posta adresini ve mesajına ya da eklerine koymayı seçtiğin bilgileri alırız. Bu yazışma, uygulama içindeki finansal kayıtlarından ayrı olarak e-posta sağlayıcımız üzerinden işlenir ve reklam ortaklarına aktarılmaz. Sorunu anlatmak için gerekli değilse hassas finansal ayrıntı veya fiş görseli göndermemeni öneririz. Destek yazışmasının silinmesi hakkında aşağıdaki adresten bize ulaşabilirsin.

Finansal tavsiye değildir

Uygulama kişisel bir kayıt tutma aracıdır. Finansal, vergi ya da muhasebe danışmanlığı vermez, resmi bir muhasebe ya da bankacılık hizmeti değildir.

Veri saklama

Uygulamaya girdiğin finansal kayıtlar için kendimize ait bir sunucu/veritabanı işletmiyoruz. Bu kayıtlar cihazında ve eşzamanlama kullanılabiliyorsa kendi özel iCloud veritabanında saklanır. Reklam ortakları ise yukarıda açıklanan teknik reklam verilerini kendi gizlilik politikalarına göre ayrıca saklayabilir; Uygulamadaki Tüm Verileri Sil işlemi ortakların elindeki verileri doğrudan silmez.
