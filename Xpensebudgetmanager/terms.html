Ads

Rewarded video

The rewarded video is optional and is offered in one place: when you export a CSV report from Analytics. If you watch it, you can export the report. If no video can be loaded or shown, or it is closed before the reward is earned, the App offers one more try; if that also fails, you can export the CSV without an ad.

Your data

We don't operate a server for your records. Keeping that data is therefore between you, your device and Apple; please see the Privacy Policy. The ad networks that show ads in the App receive some technical data from your device; the Privacy Policy explains what, and how to change your ad choice.

Reports and CSV exports

A CSV report can contain your financial entries, including notes and merchant names. When you export one, you choose where it goes through the iOS share sheet. We don't receive it. You are responsible for where you send or save the file, who can see it, and what you do with it.

Exchange rates

When an entry is in a currency other than your main currency, the App can convert it using exchange rates fetched from api.frankfurter.dev (European Central Bank reference rates). The rates are for information only. We don't guarantee that they are accurate, complete or current, and a saved rate may be older than today's rate. Please don't rely on them for large or official transactions.

Receipt scanning

Receipt scanning reads text on your device and suggests an amount, date and merchant. It can make mistakes. You are responsible for checking the suggestions and correcting them before you save.
