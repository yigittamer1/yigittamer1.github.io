01 — English

UnitConverter ("the App") is a unit conversion utility developed by an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

No account, no personal profile

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use it. There are no user profiles.

What stays on your device

Your category and unit preferences, favorites, and selected language are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. This information is never transmitted to us and we have no access to it.

Network requests the App makes

PurposeDestinationData sent
Currency exchange ratesopen.er-api.comNone — a public rate lookup only.
AdvertisingGoogle AdMobDevice/advertising identifiers as needed to serve ads. See Google's own privacy policy below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner advertisements served by Google AdMob to support free use of the App. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Since we do not operate any backend server or database, we do not retain any of your data. Deleting the App from your device removes all locally stored preferences immediately.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to yigittamer99@gmail.com.
