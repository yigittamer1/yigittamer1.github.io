01 — English

Metrik ("the App") is a unit conversion and calculator utility developed by an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. Metrik is available in 17 languages; this policy applies regardless of which language you use the App in.

No account, no personal profile

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use it. There are no user profiles.

What stays on your device

Your category and unit preferences, favorites, recent conversions, selected language, and appearance (light/dark) setting are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. This information is never transmitted to us and we have no access to it.

Advertising, and every SDK that comes with it

The App is supported by advertising so it can remain free to use. Ads are served through AppLovin MAX, which mediates demand from two networks — Unity Ads and Yandex Ads. Each network's SDK brings along its own sub-components (for ad-quality measurement or crash reporting); every one of them ships an Apple-mandated privacy manifest, and this table reflects exactly what those manifests declare — not a guess:

SDKWhy it's hereWhat it declares collecting
AppLovin SDKAd mediation — picks which network's ad to showDevice/advertising identifiers to serve ads
Unity Ads, incl. ironSource Ad QualityOne of the two ad networksDevice ID and performance/diagnostic data, for ad-quality analytics and app functionality — not linked to your identity
Yandex Ads, incl. its AppMetrica and KSCrash componentsThe other ad networkDevice/advertising identifiers for ads; separately, Yandex's own AppMetrica toolkit collects crash and performance data, general usage data, and approximate (typically IP-based, not GPS) location for its own analytics — not linked to your identity

None of these declare themselves as "tracking" under Apple's own definition. We still ask for App Tracking Transparency permission (below) because ad mediation can involve sharing device identifiers with more than one company — you get the same result either way, we just default to asking.

The App itself never asks for your camera, photos, precise location, contacts, or microphone — it has no feature that would need them.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the App asks for Apple's standard tracking permission a short moment after launch. Whether you allow or decline tracking, every feature of the App works exactly the same — declining only limits how personalized the ads you see can be.

Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights (EEA, UK, and similar regions)

We ourselves don't hold any personal data about you — nothing we collect identifies you, and there's no account or server-side profile to access, correct, or delete. Any data collection that could count as "personal data" under GDPR/UK GDPR happens inside the advertising SDKs described above, on their own servers. To exercise access, correction, deletion, portability, or objection rights over that data, use the relevant network's own privacy tools (linked above) or contact them directly; you can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority at any time.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell your personal information, and we have no mechanism to do so — we don't collect any ourselves. Ad partners may process device/advertising identifiers as described above; declining App Tracking Transparency limits this. You can reach us at the contact address below with any CCPA-related request.

Data retention and deletion

Since we do not operate any backend server or database, we do not retain any of your data. Deleting the App from your device removes all locally stored preferences immediately.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
