Privacy Policy

Sudoku - Daily Brain Puzzles · Last updated 26 September 2026

Türkçe English

The short version: there is no account, and your puzzles and progress are never sent to us. They sync to your own private iCloud account, which only you can read. There are no ads, nothing to buy, and no third-party software in the app that collects data about you.

No account, no sign-up

The app does not create an account for you and never asks for your name, email address or password. You can play everything without identifying yourself in any way.

What stays on your device

  • Your in-progress puzzle, statistics, streaks, awards, coins and items.
  • Your settings, such as theme, language and whether the timer is shown.
  • How many free hints you have used in the puzzle you are playing.

iCloud sync

So your progress survives a new phone, the app stores that same data in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit and iCloud Key-Value Storage. This is your personal iCloud account. We have no access to it and cannot read it. If you are not signed in to iCloud the app works normally and keeps everything on the device instead.

You can erase all of it — on the device and in iCloud — at any time with Settings → Delete All Data inside the app.

No advertising, no tracking

The app contains no ads and no advertising or analytics software of any kind. It does not use the advertising identifier, does not ask for Apple's tracking permission, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

The only outside services the app talks to are Apple's: iCloud, for the sync described above, and Apple's push notification service, which carries a silent signal from iCloud telling your other devices that new data is waiting. The signal contains none of your data and shows nothing on screen. The reminders you can turn on are different: they are scheduled on your own device and go through no server at all. All of it is governed by your relationship with Apple.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive or store your puzzles, stats or progress.
  • We do not add analytics or crash reporting of our own. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass us anonymous, aggregate crash and usage statistics. They cannot tell us who you are and contain none of your puzzles or progress.
  • Sharing a puzzle or your stats sends nothing to us. The link holds only a puzzle number and a difficulty, and it goes wherever you choose to send it through the iOS share sheet.
  • If someone opens a shared link and has the app, iOS opens the puzzle in the app directly. If they do not, their browser loads a page from GitHub Pages, which hosts this site; like any web host, GitHub may log standard connection details such as an IP address. That page runs no analytics, sets no cookies and loads nothing from anyone else.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not let anyone else collect data through the app.
  • We do not sell data to anyone, because we do not hold any.
  • We do not ask for contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not sell anything, so there is no payment information to see.

Notifications

Reminders are optional. The app asks for permission once, right after you finish a puzzle, and only if you tap Remind Me. Saying no changes nothing else in the app.

If you allow them, the app schedules them on your device: up to three a day (morning, lunchtime and early evening), a few days ahead at a time, and none for the rest of a day once you have played. The evening one appears only when you have a streak to lose. There is no server involved and nothing about your play is sent anywhere. Turn them off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Sudoku.

Your rights over your data

Because there is no account and we hold nothing about you, there is no file for us to hand over or delete — everything is on your device and in your own iCloud account, and Settings → Delete All Data erases it from both at any time, without asking us.

If you have a question about any of this, write to us and a person will answer: yigittamer99@gmail.com

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.

iCloud senkronizasyonu

İlerlemeniz yeni bir telefonda da kaybolmasın diye uygulama aynı veriyi Apple'ın CloudKit ve iCloud Key-Value Storage teknolojisiyle sizin özel iCloud veritabanınızda saklar. Tamamını — cihazdaki ve iCloud'daki — istediğiniz zaman uygulama içindeki Ayarlar → Tüm Verileri Sil ile silebilirsiniz.

Reklam yok, takip yok

Uygulamada reklam yoktur ve hiçbir tür reklam ya da analitik yazılımı içermez. Reklam kimliğini kullanmaz, Apple'ın takip iznini istemez ve sizi başka şirketlerin uygulama veya sitelerinde takip etmez.

Uygulamanın konuştuğu tek dış hizmetler Apple'ınkilerdir: yukarıda anlatılan senkronizasyon için iCloud ve Apple'ın anlık bildirim hizmeti; bu hizmet iCloud'dan diğer cihazlarınıza "yeni veri var" diyen sessiz bir işaret taşır. Bu işaret sizin verinizden hiçbir şey içermez ve ekranda hiçbir şey göstermez. Açabileceğiniz hatırlatmalar ise farklıdır: kendi cihazınızda planlanır ve hiçbir sunucudan geçmez. Hepsi Apple ile aranızdaki ilişkiye tabidir.

Bildirimler

Hatırlatmalar isteğe bağlıdır. Uygulama izni bir kez, bir bulmacayı bitirdikten hemen sonra ve yalnızca Hatırlat'a dokunursanız sorar. Hayır demek uygulamada başka hiçbir şeyi değiştirmez.

İzin verirseniz hatırlatmalar cihazınızda planlanır: günde en fazla üç tane (sabah, öğle ve akşamüstü), bir seferde birkaç gün ilerisi için, ve o gün oynadıktan sonra günün geri kalanında hiçbiri. Akşam hatırlatması yalnızca kaybedecek bir seriniz varsa çıkar. Araya hiçbir sunucu girmez ve oyununuza dair hiçbir şey bir yere gönderilmez. İstediğiniz zaman iOS Ayarlar → Bildirimler → Sudoku'dan kapatabilirsiniz.
