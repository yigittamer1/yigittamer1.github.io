Purchases

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and are charged to your Apple Account. The app offers:

No puzzle, difficulty level or game mode is locked behind a purchase.

Sudoku Plus subscription

  • What it includes: no banner or full-screen ads, unlimited hints, a daily coin allowance, access to past Daily Challenges, and detailed statistics.
  • Length and price: offered as a monthly or a yearly subscription. The exact price for your country is always shown in the app before you confirm, and is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Free trial: first-time subscribers to the yearly plan get a 7-day free trial. Apple allows one introductory offer per person per subscription group. If you subscribe before the trial ends, any unused part of the trial is forfeited.
  • Auto-renewal: the subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Managing and cancelling: you can manage the subscription and turn off auto-renew at any time in your Apple Account settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not shorten or refund the period you already paid for.
