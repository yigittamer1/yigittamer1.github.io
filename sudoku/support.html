Need a hand? Email yigittamer99@gmail.com. One person reads it, so please say which iPhone and iOS version you are on — it usually saves a round trip.

My progress disappeared

Progress lives in your own private iCloud account. If it is missing on a new device, check that you are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is on. The app keeps working with iCloud off, but it then stores everything only on that one device.

I bought something and it did not unlock

Open the shop or the Sudoku Plus screen and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that made them, so this has to be the same account. If it still does not appear, email us with the date of the purchase and we will help.

How do I cancel Sudoku Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app: open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals and you keep Plus until the period you paid for ends.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A rewarded ad did not give me my reward

The reward is granted when the ad finishes. If the video was closed early, or the connection dropped partway, it does not count. If an ad played all the way through and nothing arrived, tell us roughly when it happened.

I think a puzzle is wrong

Every puzzle is machine-checked to have exactly one solution before it is ever shown, so this is worth reporting. Use Share on that puzzle and send us the link — it identifies the exact board.

Turning off notifications

iOS Settings → Sudoku → Notifications. The app only ever schedules reminders on your device; nothing is sent from a server.

Deleting everything

In the app: Settings → Delete All Data. It erases your progress on the device and in your iCloud account. Purchases are not deleted by this, because they belong to your Apple Account — use Restore Purchases to get them back.
